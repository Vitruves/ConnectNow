ConnectNow

Mac struggling to connect to public Wi-Fi? Here’s the fix.

Hotel, train station, café, airport: you're connected, but nothing loads and the login page never shows up. ConnectNow finds it and opens it.

Download for Mac Source on GitHub

Free and open source. macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel. The app isn't notarized: the first time, right-click it and choose Open.

Why the login page doesn't show up

macOS only opens the Wi-Fi login window when its own check gets redirected by the network. Plenty of ordinary settings stop that from happening, and then you're stuck: connected, but offline.

ConnectNow looks for the page in other ways, so it works even when macOS gives up.

  • A VPN or iCloud Private RelayYour traffic leaves through the tunnel, so the hotspot never gets to redirect it.
  • A firewall, ad blocker or custom DNSSomething on your Mac filters the very requests the network needs to see.
  • A web proxy or another connectionEthernet or your iPhone's hotspot takes priority over the Wi-Fi.
  • A picky portalSome networks let Apple's check through and block everything else.

What ConnectNow does

Finds the page

It checks the same addresses Apple, Google, Firefox and Microsoft use, asks the network directly, and goes around your VPN when it has to.

Opens it

The login page opens in its own window, with the real address always visible. Nothing is kept once you leave the network.

Tells you what's wrong

VPN, proxy, private Wi-Fi address, a filter running in the background: you see what's in the way and what to do about it.

Fixes the usual suspect

When the cause can be fixed for you, one click does it. Your settings come back as soon as you're online.

Keeps watch

It rescans when the network changes and notices when your session expires, so the login page comes back on its own.

Stays out of your business

No account, no tracking. It only contacts the connectivity checks, the login page, and GitHub to look for updates.

Also in the terminal

Right-click in the window and choose Install Command-Line Tool. Then:

connectnow          # status, login page address, what's blocking it
connectnow watch    # one line each time something changes
connectnow open     # open the login page in your browser
connectnow --json   # for scripts

Keep it in your Applications folder for the next trip.

Open it whenever a network won't let you in.
